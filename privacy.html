Inbox Agent privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026.

Who uses it

Inbox Agent is used by one person only: the owner of the Gmail account it is connected to. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the permissions the owner granted through Google's sign-in screen, Inbox Agent reads the messages, threads, labels and drafts in her Gmail account using the Gmail API.

What it does with that access

It reads new messages to decide which of her labels fits and whether a reply is needed. It applies labels and creates reply drafts. It does not send, forward or delete email. Every reply is sent by the owner herself.

Where the data goes

Email content is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to choose labels and write drafts. Notes the tool keeps about how the owner sorts and answers her mail are stored in her private GitHub repository, and the hourly job runs on GitHub Actions in that repository. The tool was set up on a computer operated by the developer who built it for her, with her permission.

Data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Google API data

Inbox Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Keeping and removing data

Labels and drafts stay in the owner's Gmail, where she can remove them at any time. She can withdraw the tool's access to her account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The notes in her repository are deleted when she deletes them or the repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
